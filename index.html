The 5 Sacred Days

Day 1: Dhanteras

Dedicated to worshipping Goddess Lakshmi and Lord Dhanvantari. People buy gold, silver, and new utensils for wealth and wellness.

Day 2: Naraka Chaturdashi (Chhoti Diwali)

Celebrates the victory of Lord Krishna over the demon Narakasura. Homes are decorated with rangolis and diyas.

Day 3: Lakshmi Puja (Main Diwali)

The main day marking Lord Rama's return to Ayodhya. Families illuminate homes with lamps and offer prayers to Lakshmi & Ganesha.

Day 4: Govardhan Puja / Padwa

Honors Lord Krishna lifting Mount Govardhan to protect villagers from torrential rains. Annakut feasts are prepared.

Day 5: Bhai Dooj

Celebrates the sacred bond between brothers and sisters, where sisters pray for their brothers' long life and prosperity.

Eco-Friendly Celebration

  • Light natural clay diyas with mustard or sesame oil.
  • Create organic Rangolis using rice powder and flower petals.
  • Choose digital fireworks or green crackers to minimize air pollution.
